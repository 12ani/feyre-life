Good to know

Trip notes

IC Get a Suica or Pasmo

Tap-and-go for every train and convenience store. Add the digital one to your phone wallet.

山 Ride the Yamanote loop

The JR loop connects Shibuya, Harajuku, Akihabara & Tokyo Station. Stay near a stop on it.

荷 Forward your luggage

Use takkyubin courier to send big bags Tokyo → Kyoto, so the Fuji day is hands-free.

予 Book ahead

teamLab, the Kawaguchiko bus, and Kyoto hotels sell out for late October. Reserve early.
