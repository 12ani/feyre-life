Good to know

Trip notes

IC Get a Suica

Tap-and-go for every train and convenience store. Before you fly, add Suica to your phone — on iPhone, use Apple Wallet or the Welcome Suica Mobile app. Can't add it? Buy a Welcome Suica card at Narita — JR East Travel Service Center, Terminal 2, B1F.

山 Ride the Yamanote loop

The JR loop connects Shibuya, Harajuku, Akihabara & Tokyo Station. Stay near a stop on it.

荷 Forward your luggage

Use takkyubin courier to send big bags Tokyo → Kyoto, so the Fuji day is hands-free.

鞄 Checked bags

Flying in (Alaska): a carry-on and 2 checked bags each are free. Flying home (Air India): only the 1st checked bag is free; a 2nd is $160.01 per person. Pack for the stricter one.

免 Shop tax-free

Bring your passports when you shop. Many stores take the tax off for tourists on purchases over ¥5,000.

予 Book ahead

The Kawaguchiko bus, Shibuya Sky sunset slots and the glänta ring workshop fill up for late October. Reserve early.
