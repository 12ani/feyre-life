Good to know

Trip notes

IC Get a Suica

Tap-and-go for every train and convenience store. Before you fly, add Suica to your phone — on iPhone, use Apple Wallet or the Welcome Suica Mobile app. Can't add it? Buy a Welcome Suica card at Narita — JR East Travel Service Center, Terminal 2, B1F.

駅 Your three stations

Higashi-ginza (Hibiya & Asakusa lines) is 3 min from the hotel, Tsukiji 4 min, Ginza 8 min. One stop to Shimbashi for the Fuji tour; Ginza → Shinjuku is 17 min on the Marunouchi line; Tokyo Station is two stops away.

荷 Forward your luggage

Hand big bags to the front desk on the Wednesday night (takkyubin) and they meet you in Kyoto, so the bullet-train day is hands-free.

鞄 Checked bags

Flying in (Alaska): a carry-on and 2 checked bags each are free. Flying home (Air India): only the 1st checked bag is free; a 2nd is $160.01 per person. Pack for the stricter one.

免 Shop tax-free

Bring your passports when you shop. Many stores take the tax off for tourists on purchases over ¥5,000.

予 Book ahead

Shibuya Sky (the 4:00 or 4:30 entry on Oct 21) and the glänta ring workshop fill up for late October. If Shibuya Sky's own site refuses your card — it often refuses foreign ones — Klook and KKday hold their own tickets.
