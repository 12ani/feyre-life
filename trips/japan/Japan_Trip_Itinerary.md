## Day-by-Day

*Times are rough guides — shift them as the day goes.*

### Day 1 — Mon Oct 19 · Tokyo (arrive)
- Land at Narita (Terminal 2) **4:00 – ~5:15 pm**
- Ramen dinner · keep it light (jet lag) 🍜 **~7:30 – 8:30 pm**

### Day 2 — Tue Oct 20 · Tokyo (teamLab + Asakusa + Akihabara)
- teamLab Planets **~9:00 – 10:45 am** — Booked ✓ Join the queue within the 30-min entry window on your ticket. You wade through water, so wear shorts or roll-up trousers.
- Asakusa — Senso-ji & Nakamise St 🍡 **~11:30 am – 2:00 pm** — ~40 min from teamLab. Tokyo's old-town neighbourhood: the oldest temple, the snack-stall street, and lunch here.
- Akihabara **~2:30 – 7:30 pm** — ~15 min on the Ginza line. GiGo & TAITO Station arcades, Super Potato (retro games), Radio Kaikan (anime & figures), a maid café. Dinner here — best once the lights come on.

### Day 3 — Wed Oct 21 · Tokyo (Mt. Fuji small-group tour)
- Meet the tour at Shimbashi **7:50 am** — One stop on the Asakusa line from Higashi-ginza. In front of the MUFG bank by the Dai-ichi Hotel — look for the orange Tourist Japan flag.
- The tour · Tourist Japan small group **7:50 am – ~6:00 pm** — Chureito Pagoda & Arakura Sengen Shrine (2 hrs) · Oishi Park, Lake Kawaguchi (2 hrs) · Oshino Hakkai (1 hr) · Mt Fuji 5th Station (1 hr) · Fujiyoshida for lunch & souvenirs 🍜 (1 hr). Order and pace are the guide’s; lunch isn’t included. The 5th Station is 2,300 m and near freezing in late October — take a warm layer; if snow closes the road they swap in another stop.
- Back at Shimbashi **~6:00 pm** — One stop home on the Asakusa line, or dinner in Ginza. Send luggage ahead to Kyoto from the hotel tonight.
- Shinjuku by night 🍢 · if you have legs left **~7:00 – 9:00 pm** — 15 min on the Yamanote from Shimbashi: Godzilla Head, the 3D billboards, Yodobashi & Kitamura camera stores, skewers at Omoide Yokocho.

### Day 4 — Thu Oct 22 · Tokyo (shrine + Harajuku & Shibuya + Shibuya Sky)
- Meiji Shrine & Yoyogi Park **~9:00 – 10:15 am** — Calm forest shrine, right beside Harajuku.
- Harajuku — Takeshita St & Omotesando 🍓 **~10:30 am – 2:15 pm** — Kiddy Land, the gacha shops (Capsule Lab by Capcom, C-pla, GachaGacha no Mori), crepes, lunch, the vintage shops in the backstreets, then Omotesando.
- Shibuya shopping **~2:45 – 5:30 pm** — ~15 min walk from Omotesando. Loft for souvenirs & stickers, the Pokémon Center and Nintendo Tokyo (both in Shibuya PARCO).
- Shibuya Crossing **~5:30 – 5:45 pm** — Walk the world's busiest crossing after dark (sunset is 5:00).
- Early dinner in Shibuya **~5:45 – 6:30 pm** — Stay close to Shibuya Scramble Square — the Sky entry window opens at 6:40.
- Shibuya Sky · night view **6:40 – ~8:00 pm** — Booked ✓ (KKday, 2 adults). Entry window is 6:40–6:59 pm only — late arrivals can be turned away. Show the QR voucher at the 14th-floor entrance of Shibuya Scramble Square; carry passports. Bags, hats & scarves go in the 46F lockers before the rooftop. No limit on how long you stay.

### Day 5 — Fri Oct 23 · Tokyo → Kyoto (Ginza + evening bullet train)
- Shop Ginza **~10:00 am – 12:00 pm** — Check out and leave bags at the front desk first. Mitsukoshi & Itoya (open 10:00 am), Seiko House (Wako) & Seiko Dream Square for Seiko watches (10:30 am), Ginza Six (10:30 am), the Uniqlo flagship (11:00 am).
- Ginza Tsuboyaki-imo 🍠 **~12:00 – 12:30 pm** — Sweet potato roasted in clay pots over charcoal. Opens at noon; closed Mondays.
- Last shops + lunch in Ginza **~12:30 – 3:30 pm**
- Collect bags from the hotel **~4:00 pm** — Tokyo Station is two stops away; be on the platform by ~4:45.
- Shinkansen · Tokyo → Kyoto **5:00 – ~7:15 pm** — Booked ✓ Nozomi, 5:00 pm from Tokyo Station.
- Check in + dinner near the hotel **~7:45 pm** — Pontocho and the Kawaramachi lanes are a short walk.

### Day 6 — Sat Oct 24 · Kyoto
- Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion) **~9:00 – 10:00 am** — Opens 9:00 am. Go early for the cleanest reflection.
- Arashiyama Bamboo Grove **~10:45 am – 2:30 pm** — ~40 min from Kinkaku-ji. Towering bamboo, riverside walk, lunch.
- Gion & Pontocho dinner **~5:00 – 8:00 pm** — Old geisha district, then the riverside dining alley — both walkable from the hotel.

### Day 7 — Sun Oct 25 · Kyoto (Nara DAY TRIP + Fushimi Inari)
- Todai-ji **~9:45 – 11:15 am** — Giant bronze Buddha.
- Nara Park **~11:15 am – 2:00 pm** — Bowing deer — buy crackers (cash). Lunch nearby, then the JR Nara line back towards Kyoto — from JR Nara station, not Kintetsu (Kintetsu doesn’t stop at Inari).
- Fushimi Inari **~2:45 – 4:30 pm** — Inari station is on the JR Nara line home from Nara — take a local train; the Miyakoji Rapid skips Inari. Thousands of vermilion torii gates; busy on a Sunday afternoon, but the crowds thin out the higher you climb. Then back to the hotel to rest.
- Dinner at Pontocho **~6:00 – 8:00 pm** — Nishiki Market is mostly shut on Sunday evenings.

### Day 8 — Mon Oct 26 · Kyoto (Kiyomizu-dera, glänta ring, Nishiki)
- Kiyomizu-dera + Higashiyama **~8:15 – 10:15 am** — Hillside temple, then down the historic Sannenzaka & Ninenzaka lanes.
- glänta Ninenzaka · make your own ring **~10:30 – 11:45 am** — Opens 10:00 am. Hammer and shape a silver ring yourself (~1 hr), take it home the same day. Book the 10:30 am slot online by the day before.
- Lunch in Gion **~12:00 – 1:00 pm**
- Nishiki Market + a tea house 🍢 **~1:30 – 4:15 pm** — "Kyoto's Kitchen" — graze, then a traditional tea experience.
- Teramachi & Shinkyogoku arcades **~4:15 – 6:00 pm** — Covered shopping streets at Nishiki's east end, right by the hotel.

### Day 9 — Tue Oct 27 · Kyoto → Osaka (travel + vintage)
- Amerikamura vintage shopping **~12:00 – 3:30 pm** — Drop bags at the Osaka hotel first (~1 hr from Kyoto by train). Lunch, then Osaka's vintage hub — Kinji and JAM are the big ones. Shops open 11:00 am.
- Dotonbori 🐙 **~5:30 – 9:00 pm** — Takoyaki, okonomiyaki, the Glico sign at night — a short walk from the hotel.

### Day 10 — Wed Oct 28 · Osaka (last full day)
- Osaka Castle **~9:30 – 11:30 am** — City views from the tower; free park grounds.
- Lunch **~12:00 – 1:00 pm**
- Den Den Town + Shinsaibashi 🥞 **~1:15 – 5:30 pm** — Osaka's anime district, then the shopping arcade. Soufflé pancakes!
- Last dinner in Dotonbori **~6:30 – 8:30 pm** — Pack tonight and sleep early — alarm ~4:30 am.

### Thu Oct 29 · Departure
- Check out → taxi to Itami **~5:00 am** — ~30 min, arrive ~5:35 am. Not KIX. Ask the front desk to book the taxi the night before — the first airport bus from Namba (6:10 am) is too late.
- Itami (ITM) → Haneda (HND) **7:05 am** — Air India, flown by ANA · 1h 5m · lands 8:10 am. No pre-reserved seats; you get them at check-in.
- 2h 20m layover at Haneda **8:10 – 10:30 am** — Domestic arrival → free shuttle to the international terminal (T3), then immigration and security. Don't linger.
- Haneda (HND) → Delhi (DEL) **10:30 am** — Air India · 787-8 Dreamliner · 10h 5m · lands 5:05 pm Delhi time.

## Transport Cheat-Sheet

- **City transit:** Google Maps gives flawless step-by-step train directions everywhere.
- **Your three stations:** Higashi-ginza (Hibiya + Asakusa lines) 3 min from the hotel, Tsukiji 4 min, Ginza 8 min. One stop to Shimbashi for the Fuji tour; Ginza → Shinjuku 17 min on the Marunouchi line; Tokyo Station two stops.
- **Shinkansen to Kyoto:** booked for 5:00 pm Fri Oct 23 from Tokyo Station, two stops from the hotel (Nozomi, ~2h15 — arrives ~7:15 pm).
- **Fuji day:** the small-group tour collects you at Shimbashi and brings you back there — no trains to work out.
- **Luggage:** hand big bags to the front desk on the Wednesday night (takkyubin) and they meet you in Kyoto, so the bullet-train day is hands-free.
