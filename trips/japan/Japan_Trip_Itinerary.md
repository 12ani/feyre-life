## Day-by-Day

### Day 1 — Mon Oct 19 · Tokyo (arrive)
- Land at Haneda, train into the city, **check into Tokyo hotel**
- Shibuya Crossing + Shibuya Sky for sunset
- Ramen dinner; keep it light for jet lag

### Day 2 — Tue Oct 20 · Tokyo (old town + anime)
- Morning: Asakusa — Senso-ji temple + Nakamise snack street (Skytree nearby)
- Afternoon/Evening: Akihabara — anime, figures, arcades, a maid café

### Day 3 — Wed Oct 21 · Tokyo (culture + shopping)
- Morning: Meiji Shrine + Yoyogi Park
- Midday: Harajuku — Takeshita Street + Omotesando shopping + crepes
- Evening: teamLab Planets, or Shinjuku by night (Golden Gai, Omoide Yokocho)
- Tonight: forward big luggage to Kyoto by courier (takkyubin); pack light

### Day 4 — Thu Oct 22 · Tokyo (Mt. Fuji DAY TRIP)
- Early highway bus from Busta Shinjuku → Kawaguchiko (~2 hrs)
- Morning: Chureito Pagoda (clearest air)
- Afternoon: lakeside / Oishi Park views
- Bus back to Tokyo — sleep in your same hotel

### Day 5 — Fri Oct 23 · Tokyo → Kyoto (travel)
- **Check out of Tokyo hotel**
- Shinkansen from Tokyo Station → Kyoto (~2h15; sit on the right for a Fuji glimpse)
- **Check into Kyoto hotel**
- Dusk: Fushimi Inari (thousand torii gates)

### Day 6 — Sat Oct 24 · Kyoto
- Morning: Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion)
- Afternoon: Arashiyama Bamboo Grove + riverside
- Evening: Gion; Pontocho alley for riverside dinner

### Day 7 — Sun Oct 25 · Kyoto (Nara DAY TRIP)
- Nara (~45 min each way): Todai-ji giant Buddha + bowing deer
- Back to Kyoto for the evening — Nishiki Market or Pontocho

### Day 8 — Mon Oct 26 · Kyoto
- Morning: Kiyomizu-dera + Higashiyama lanes (Ninenzaka / Sannenzaka)
- Afternoon: Nishiki Market + a traditional tea house experience

### Day 9 — Tue Oct 27 · Kyoto → Osaka (travel)
- **Check out of Kyoto hotel**
- Quick train Kyoto → Osaka (~15–30 min)
- **Check into Osaka hotel**
- Afternoon/Evening: Dotonbori — takoyaki, okonomiyaki, the Glico sign

### Day 10 — Wed Oct 28 · Osaka (last full day)
- Morning: Osaka Castle
- Afternoon: Den Den Town (anime) + Shinsaibashi shopping
- Evening: last desserts (soufflé pancakes!), Dotonbori night

### Thu Oct 29 · Departure
- **Check out of Osaka hotel**
- Train to Kansai Airport / KIX (~50 min)
- Morning flight to Delhi (lands same evening)

## Transport Cheat-Sheet

- **City transit:** Google Maps gives flawless step-by-step train directions everywhere.
- **Yamanote line:** the JR loop connecting Shibuya, Harajuku, Akihabara, Ueno, Tokyo Station — stay near a Yamanote stop for easy access.
- **Shinkansen to Kyoto:** departs Tokyo Station or Shinagawa (both on the Yamanote loop). Fastest train = Nozomi (~2h15).
- **Fuji day:** highway bus from Busta Shinjuku (Level 4, Shinjuku Station south side) → Kawaguchiko. Book ahead; go early.
- **Luggage:** use takkyubin courier to send big bags Tokyo → Kyoto so you travel light on the Fuji day.
