# Orange Upside-Down Cake

Not copied from anywhere — a plain creamed butter cake built over caramel and
blanched orange slices. Makes one 8-inch round cake.

## Ingredients

**For the top**

- 2 medium thin-skinned oranges, sliced 3mm thin
- 55g unsalted butter
- 90g light brown sugar

**For the cake**

- 115g unsalted butter, softened
- 150g caster sugar
- zest of 2 more oranges (about 8g)
- 2 large eggs
- 160g plain flour
- 40g ground almonds
- 1½ tsp baking powder
- ½ tsp fine salt
- 100g plain yogurt or sour cream
- 50g orange juice, squeezed from the zested two
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

## Baking

175°C / 350°F for 45–55 minutes.

## Method

1. **Prep** — Heat the oven. Butter the tin and line its base with parchment; the caramel goes on top of the paper.
2. **Blanch** — Slice the oranges thin, flick out the pips, simmer the slices in water for 2 minutes and drain them flat on a tea towel. This takes the bitterness out of the peel.
3. **Caramel** — Melt the butter with the brown sugar over low heat until smooth and just bubbling. Pour into the tin and tilt to cover the base.
4. **Arrange** — Lay the slices over the caramel, overlapping, from the outside in. Press them flat.
5. **Zest** — Rub the zest into the caster sugar, then beat it with the softened butter for 3–4 minutes until pale and fluffy.
6. **Eggs** — Beat in the eggs one at a time, then the vanilla.
7. **Fold** — Whisk the flour, almonds, baking powder and salt together. Fold in a third, then half the yogurt and juice; repeat, finishing with the dry.
8. **Bake** — Spoon the batter over the oranges, level it, and bake 45–55 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean.
9. **Turn out** — Rest 10–15 minutes, loosen the edge, and invert while still warm. Cold caramel sets like glue.
10. **Finish** — Peel off the parchment, press back any stray slice, and brush over the syrup left in the tin.

*Thin-skinned oranges only — blood oranges make the prettiest version. No ground almonds: use 40g more flour and an extra spoonful of yogurt.*
