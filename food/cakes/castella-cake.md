# Castella Cake

Tall, bouncy Taiwanese-style castella, based on [@emojoiecuisine](https://www.instagram.com/emojoiecuisine/)'s recipe. Makes one 6×6 square (15 cm) cake.

## Ingredients

- 60g (4 Tbsp) unsalted butter
- 60g (¼ cup) milk
- 4 egg yolks
- 65g (½ cup) cake flour, sifted
- 3g (½ tsp) vanilla extract (optional)
- 1g (a pinch) salt
- 4 egg whites
- 65g (⅓ cup) sugar

## Baking

150°C / 300°F for 50–60 minutes, until the top is brown.

## Method

1. **Prep** — Line the pan with parchment, leaving the paper standing taller than the sides. Heat the oven.
2. **Warm** — Melt the butter with the milk over low heat until it just steams.
3. **Paste** — Pour it over the sifted cake flour and whisk until smooth.
4. **Yolks** — Whisk in the egg yolks, vanilla and salt.
5. **Meringue** — Whip the egg whites with the sugar, added in three goes, to soft–medium peaks.
6. **Fold** — Loosen the batter with a third of the meringue, then gently fold in the rest.
7. **Pan** — Pour into the pan and tap out large bubbles.
8. **Bake** — 150°C for 50–60 minutes, until the top is brown.
9. **Cool** — Lift out by the paper and peel the sides away. It sinks a little as it cools.

*Optional: bake in a water bath (pan set in about 2 cm of hot water) for a moister crumb and a crack-free top.*
