# Rich Chocolate Ganache Filling

Adjusted for 7 servings.

A thick, spreadable ganache that holds its shape between cake layers. Fills an 8- or 9-inch cake with 2–3 layers.

## Ingredients

- 6⅛ oz dark chocolate (60–70%), finely chopped
- ½ cup heavy cream
- ⅝ oz unsalted butter, softened
- ⅝ tbsp light corn syrup or honey (optional, for shine)
- ⅛ tsp fine salt
- ⅝ tsp vanilla extract

## Method

1. Finely chop the dark chocolate and place it in a heatproof bowl. Small, even pieces melt smoothly.
2. Warm the heavy cream and corn syrup (or honey) in a saucepan over medium heat until it just begins to simmer around the edges. Don't let it boil hard.
3. Pour the hot cream over the chocolate and leave it untouched so the heat softens the chocolate.
4. Stir gently from the centre outward with a spatula until smooth and glossy. Add the butter, salt and vanilla extract and stir until fully combined.
5. Leave at room temperature, stirring occasionally, until thick like peanut butter. For faster setting, refrigerate and stir every 10 minutes.
6. Spread an even layer (about ½ cm thick) on each cooled cake layer, stack, and chill the cake for 30 minutes to firm up before frosting or serving.

## Notes

For a lighter filling, whip the cooled (but not set) ganache with a mixer for 2–3 minutes until fluffy and paler. If it splits or looks greasy, whisk in 1–2 tbsp warm cream. Using milk chocolate? Increase the chocolate to about a 2.5:1 ratio with the cream, as it sets softer. Make sure cake layers are completely cool before filling.
