# Sourdough Focaccia

Airy, olive-oil-rich sourdough focaccia baked in a 9×13 pan. Sticky dough, no kneading — just folds, patience, and a good dimpling. Makes one 9×13 pan (about 12 servings).

## Ingredients

- 125g active sourdough starter (about ½ cup)
- 380g water (1½ cups + 1 Tbsp)
- 500g unbleached bread flour or AP flour (4 cups)
- 15g olive oil (1 Tbsp), plus more for the pan and top
- 5g garlic salt (1 tsp)
- 5g regular salt (1 tsp)
- 1 Tbsp butter, for greasing the pan

*Salt swap: You can use 10g regular salt total instead of splitting garlic + regular salt.*

## Instructions

1. **Mix** — Add starter, water, and olive oil to a bowl and mix until fully combined. Add flour and salt, then mix until no dry flour remains. The dough will be sticky.
2. **Rest** — Cover and rest at room temperature for 45 minutes.
3. **Stretch & Folds** — Do 2 sets of stretch and folds, 30 minutes apart. Cover the bowl between each set.
4. **Coil Folds** — Do 2 sets of coil folds, 30 minutes apart. Cover the bowl between each set.
5. **Bulk Ferment** — Let the dough finish fermenting at room temperature until it doubles in size.
6. **Pan** — Grease a 9×13 pan with butter and drizzle with olive oil. Gently dump the dough into the pan. Cover and rest for 1 hour.
7. **Dimple & Top** — Drizzle the dough with olive oil, then dimple all over with oiled or wet fingers. Add your desired toppings.
8. **Bake** — Bake at 400°F for 30–35 minutes, until golden.

## Optional Overnight Cold Proof

- After bulk ferment, cover the dough in the bowl and refrigerate overnight.
- When ready to bake, let it sit at room temperature for 2 hours.
- Transfer to the pan, rest 1 hour, then dimple and bake.
